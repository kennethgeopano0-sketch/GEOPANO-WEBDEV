Our Story

Nestled in the heart of the city, BOXYARD Resto Bar blends sophisticated cuisine with a vibrant bar scene. Our chef-curated menu features seasonal ingredients, while our mixologists craft signature cocktails that delight the senses.

Enjoy live jazz performances, cozy leather booths, and impeccable service that makes every visit unforgettable.
